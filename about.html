About BorrowBox

What You Need. When You Need It.

BorrowBox is a convenient web-based platform designed to enable individuals to access items or equipment at a cheaper price or for free for a specified period. Our goal is to make sharing campus resources easier, more affordable, and efficient for everyone.

Who We Are

BorrowBox is a campus-based project that connects people who are willing to lend items to those who need to borrow them. Items can range from electronic devices, scientific calculators, textbooks, chargers, and much more.

Our Vision

To become a trusted platform that promotes resource sharing, sustainability, and stronger campus relationships.
