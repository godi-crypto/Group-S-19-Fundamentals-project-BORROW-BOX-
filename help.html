Help & Support

Got questions? We're here to help.

How do I borrow an item?

Navigate to the Home page, browse or search for the item you need, and submit a borrowing request to the owner.

How do I list an item to lend?

Go to your "My Lendings" tab to add and manage items you are willing to share with fellow students.

Need to contact the team?

Reach out to any of our team members listed on the About Us page for technical support.
