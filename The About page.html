About Us
What You Need. When You Need It.

BorrowBox is a convenient web-based platform designed to enable individuals to access certain items or requirements at a cheaper price or for free for a specified period of time. Our goal is to make sharing resources easier, more affordable, and efficient for everyone.

Who We Are

BorrowBox is a campus-based project that connects people who are willing to lend items to those who want to borrow them. The item can be an electronic device, a book, textbook, charger, and so many others.

Our Vision

To become a trusted platform that promotes resource sharing, sustainability, and stronger relationships.
