About Us

What You Need. When You Need It.

BorrowBox is a convenient web-based platform designed to help people access items at a lower price or for free for a specified period. Our goal is to make sharing resources easier, more affordable, and efficient.

Who We Are

BorrowBox is a campus-based project connecting people willing to lend items with those who want to borrow them. Items can include electronics, books, textbooks, chargers, and more.

Our Vision

To become a trusted platform that promotes resource sharing, sustainability, and stronger relationships.
